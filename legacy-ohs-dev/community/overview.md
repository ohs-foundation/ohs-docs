### Join the OHS community

Become a part of a global network of developers, implementers, and healthcare professionals working together to accelerate digital health transformation. OHS is an open community—started from a [collaboration with the World Health Organization and developers](https://blog.google/technology/health/health-developer-tool-thecheckup/)—focused on building and supporting interoperable health solutions using FHIR standards. Now it's your turn to get involved!

Connect with fellow contributors, learn from experts, and find ways to get involved. Together, we can make a difference in global health.

[code](https://developers.google.com/open-health-stack/community/contribute)
