# Join our developer calls

Join our weekly Open Health Stack developer calls, held every Thursday, to
engage directly with the OHS engineering team and community.

We alternate between **OHS client** (Android FHIR SDK) and **OHS server-side**
(FHIR Data Pipes and Info Gateway) topics each week.

To be added to the calls, email: [developers@ohs.community](mailto:developers@ohs.community)
