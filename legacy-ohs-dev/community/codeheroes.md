# Open Health Stack  |  Google for Developers

### Recognizing Open Health Stack Code Heroes

The Open Health Stack community recognizes the code heroes who have contributed to Open Health Stack.

Code heroes are individuals who have made significant contributions to the Open Health Stack project, and have gone above and beyond in their work. They are recognized for their contributions, and are rewarded with a badge and a mention on this page.
